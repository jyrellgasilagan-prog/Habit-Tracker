    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: October 24, 2026. This policy describes how Habit Tracker handles information. It is a student project, so please use made-up or test data and avoid entering anything sensitive.

    What the app collects and why

    Habit Tracker has no accounts, sign-in, or forms that send data to the developer. The only information involved is what you type in: habit names, the icon, color, and weekly goal you choose, and the days you check off. The app uses it to show your habits, streaks, history, and insights.

    How information is stored and shared

    • Your habit data is stored in your own browser (localStorage) on your device. The app does not upload it to any server, and the developer cannot see it.
    • The app does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies, and your data is not sold or shared.
    • Google Fonts: the page loads its fonts from fonts.googleapis.com. Google may receive your IP address and browser details when the fonts load, under Google's own privacy policy.
    • Hosting: the site is served by its hosting provider (GitHub Pages), which may keep standard server logs such as your IP address and the time of your visit.
    • If you use “Download backup,” a file is saved to your device. Where you keep or send that file is up to you.

    Deleting your information

    Because your data stays on your device, you control it. Press “Delete everything” in the controls above, or delete a single habit with its Delete button. You can also clear this site's data in your browser settings. There is nothing stored on a server for the developer to delete.

    Safeguards and limits

    Your habit data stays on your device and is never sent over the internet by this app. It is not encrypted, though, so anyone who can use your browser profile or device can view it. Clearing your browser data, or using private browsing, will erase it unless you saved a backup.
